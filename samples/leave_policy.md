# Employee Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at two days per month. Up to 10 unused days may be carried over to the next year; any remaining days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave exceeds three consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried over.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks. Parental leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## How to request leave
Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance for absences longer than five days. Managers must approve or reject requests within three working days.
